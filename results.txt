PROJECT: Fault-Injection Evaluation of Triple Modular Redundancy (TMR)

Experiment:
Comparison of an unprotected accumulator and a TMR-protected accumulator
under single-register fault injection.

Input sequence: 5, 3, 2
Correct final accumulator value: 10
Injected fault value: 136

Test 1: Fault in Register A
Baseline output during fault: 136
TMR output during fault: 8
TMR final output: 10
Result: PASS

Test 2: Fault in Register B
Baseline output during fault: 136
TMR output during fault: 8
TMR final output: 10
Result: PASS

Test 3: Fault in Register C
Baseline output during fault: 136
TMR output during fault: 8
TMR final output: 10
Result: PASS

Conclusion:
The simulation demonstrates that majority voting masks a single
register fault in each of the three tested TMR registers.
The unprotected baseline retains the injected corruption and produces
an incorrect final result.
